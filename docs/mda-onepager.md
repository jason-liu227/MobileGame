## One-line pitch
Players take on the role of a fearless courier sprinting through a dangerous 2D world, dodging obstacles and leaping over hazards to deliver their package as far as possible while chasing a higher score.

## Aesthetics (what the player feels)
- Tension and adrenaline as the world speeds up and obstacles become harder to avoid.
- Mastery as the player learns to react quickly and chain movements together.
- Determination from pushing further each run and beating their previous high score.
- Excitement and relief when narrowly escaping an obstacle.

## Core mechanics (3 to 5 verbs or systems)
1. Run — automatically move forward through the level.
2. Jump — leap over gaps and obstacles.
3. Slide — duck under hazards and obstacles.
4. Dodge — move around incoming threats.

## Dynamics (what emerges when the mechanics meet the player)
- Players develop a rhythm of jumping, sliding, and dodging as they learn to anticipate obstacles.
- Players take greater risks to collect coins or bonuses, balancing reward against the chance of ending their run.
- As the speed increases, players rely on quick reactions and memorised obstacle patterns to survive longer.
- Players try to beat their previous distance and score, creating a repeat-and-improve loop.

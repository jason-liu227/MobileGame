## Privacy and Consent

- Anonymous responses: Verified
- Respondent names collected: No
- Respondent email addresses collected: No
- Microsoft Forms setting: Anyone can respond
- Consent question: Included and required
- Purpose: University game development testing and quality improvement

Testers are instructed not to include personal information in their responses.

## Survey Questions

1. **Device model and Android version**
   - Choice question with known devices and an Other option.

2. **Build ID**
   - Short text field for the eight-character build ID displayed on the About screen.

3. **Installation result**
   - Worked first time
   - Needed help
   - Failed
   - Additional text field for installation error messages.

4. **Minutes played**
   - Numeric response.

5. **Crashes, freezes or not responding**
   - None
   - Freeze
   - Crash
   - Not responding
   - Additional text field describing when the issue occurred.

6. **Performance and feel (1–5 Likert scale)**
   - Smoothness
   - Heat
   - Battery drain
   - Control responsiveness

7. **Gameplay loop (1–5 Likert scale)**
   - I understood the goal.
   - I wanted one more go.
   - Difficulty felt fair.

8. **One thing to fix**
   - Free-text response.

9. **One thing to keep**
   - Free-text response.

10. **Consent**
    - Required agreement to the use of anonymous feedback for a university project.

## Feedback Analysis

Survey responses will be exported to CSV and stored in:

`docs/CA3/qa/survey/`

After collecting at least 10 responses, findings and proposed improvements will be documented in:

`docs/CA3/qa/survey/insights.md`

The analysis will contain 5–8 insights, each with an associated improvement or fix.

# Known Issues — Dashline

**Version:** 0.8.0
**Platform:** Android  
**Test device:** Samsung Galaxy S25 FE (Android 16)

## Known Issues

No known gameplay-blocking bugs or crashes were identified during the completed Android testing.

## Testing Completed

- Pause and resume testing completed successfully.
- Background and foreground transitions tested.
- Force-stop and relaunch tested with saved progress retained.
- Six Week 8 Android resilience scenarios passed.
- No crashes, freezes or ANRs were observed during the recorded tests.

## Limitations

- Testing was primarily performed on a Samsung Galaxy S25 FE; behaviour on other Android devices has not been fully verified.
- The Week 9 manual Low-tier override has not been independently confirmed in Android logcat.
- The game uses minimalist geometric placeholder-style art rather than a fully illustrated asset set.

These limitations are not confirmed bugs but identify areas for further testing and improvement.
